Privacy Policy - Dungeon Magic VR

ADA Software, LLC · Last updated November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ADA Software, LLC ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Dungeon Magic VR (the "game") for Meta Quest and PC VR.

Information We Collect

The game does not collect personal information. It has no account or sign-in, no ads, and no analytics. It does not send your data to us or to any third party.

Hand Tracking

The game uses your headset's hand tracking to read the shape and position of your hands so you can cast spells. This data is used only on your device (the headset, or the PC it is connected to) while you play. The game does not save it and does not send it anywhere.

Data Saved on Your Device

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Meta Platform

Meta handles the information it collects through its own devices and store under its own policy. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Changes take effect when the updated policy is posted on this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact form.
